# Kemy's Furniture Mod

This mod adds multiple furniture pieces to the game, spread throughout the various capitals. Each region has its own signature style.

## Items

Each region capital city has its own furniture store.

### All Regions - Capital cities
- Beds - Stackable for easier use with virtual crew
- Navigation desks - For laying out maps and other items
- Cabinets - Small, wide and large variants. Each provides internal storage for your items.
- Chests - Smaller storage chests for your items, no more storing things in old crates!
- Scroll shelves - A place to store your maps and items, visually fills as more items are added
- Carpets - Red, green and blue. Wall mountable!

### Al'Ankh - Gold Rock City
- Cushions - Available in red and white

### Aestrin - Fort Aestrin
- Captains desk - Includes storage and a desktop. Ideal for those European vibes.

### Emerald Archipelago
- Room dividers - for seperating parts of your rooms
- Chair - For sitting!
